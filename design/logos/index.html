tgOS logo options

Each option shows the full lockup on the log-on screen's blue, then the standalone mark at favicon and taskbar sizes on light and Start-bar backgrounds.

1 Luna Orb

A glossy blue sphere with a green Start-button glow along the bottom. The wordmark borrows the XP trick of one white word and one orange one.

2 Bliss Tile

A tile showing our own hillside from the wallpaper, so the logo and the desktop look like one family. Reads well even at favicon size.

3 Little Window

A miniature XP window: blue title bar, caption buttons, and the initials in the body. It is the most literal "OS" of the four.

4 Green Key

A glossy rounded key in the Start-button green with the initials on it, plus a wordmark with a dark outline and an orange swoosh.
