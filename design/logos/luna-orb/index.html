tgOS: Luna Orb iterations

The original (dashed) is first for comparison; A to D each change one thing about it. Each shows the lockup on the log-on screen's blue, then the mark at 64, 32 and 16px on light and Start-bar backgrounds.

Original Luna Orb

For comparison: the blue sphere with a green glow along the bottom, white "tg", and a white-and-orange wordmark.

A Bliss Globe

What changed: the inside. The sphere shows our wallpaper hillside, with sky and clouds above and grass below, so it reads as a tiny world.

B Orbit

What changed: the ring. A tilted orange ring loops around a slightly smaller sphere, which says "OS" and adds the XP orange accent to the mark.

C Power Orb

What changed: the symbol. A power-button ring surrounds the letters, and the wordmark gets a dark outline so it punches harder on the blue.

D Glass Bubble

What changed: the material and layout. A pale glass bubble with deep-blue letters and a gold "OS", stacked vertically like the log-on screen.
