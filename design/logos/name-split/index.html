tgOS: avoiding the repeated "tg"

The current logo (dashed) says "tg" twice. Options 1 and 2 let the orb be the "tg" and add only "OS"; options 3 and 4 say the name once in the wordmark and give the orb a different job.

Current Orb with "tg" + "tgOS"

For comparison: "tg" appears in the orb and again in the wordmark.

1 Orb + "OS"

The orb is the "tg"; the wordmark adds only a large orange "OS", so the whole thing reads "tg·OS" once. The standalone mark is unchanged.

2 Orb + "OS" badge

Same idea, but "OS" sits in a glossy orange badge like a Start button, a nod to XP's orange "xp". Reads "tg" then "OS" once.

3 Letterless orb + "tgOS"

The orb loses its letters and becomes a pure glossy planet; the name appears once, in the wordmark. The standalone mark is the plain planet.

4 Cursor orb + "tgOS"

A white mouse pointer replaces the letters, the most "operating system" symbol there is, and the name appears once in the wordmark.
